Our delivery process

From farm to your door
before 8:30 AM

ഫാമിൽ നിന്ന് നേരെ നിങ്ങളുടെ വീട്ടിലേക്ക്

While you sleep, we're already at work. Here's what happens before your morning chai.

3:00 AM Step 1

Bathing & milking

കുളിപ്പിക്കലും കറവയും

Our cows are bathed and cleaned, and milking begins at 3 AM every single day.

4:30 AM Step 2

Collection & quality check

ശേഖരണവും ഗുണനിലവാര പരിശോധനയും

Milk is collected and quality-checked to guarantee freshness, purity and safety.

4:45 – 5:30 AM Step 3

Hygienic packing

ശുചിത്വമുള്ള പാക്കിങ്

Packed hygienically into eco-friendly bottles and pouches, sealed and ready to go.

5:45 – 8:30 AM Step 4

Doorstep delivery

വീട്ടുപടിക്കൽ ഡെലിവറി

Our delivery team heads out so fresh milk reaches your door on time, every morning.

Every day Step 5

Bottle washing

കുപ്പികൾ കഴുകി വൃത്തിയാക്കൽ

Returned bottles are thoroughly washed and sterilised, ready for the next morning's milk.

What time is the milk delivered?

Deliveries go out between 5:45 AM and 8:30 AM, so your milk is at the door before breakfast.

Is the milk processed in any way?

No. Palkkaran milk is pure, unprocessed and farm fresh. It is quality-checked every morning before packing.

How do I start a daily subscription?

Download the Palkkaran app from Google Play, pick your milk and quantity, and choose a subscription. You can also message us on WhatsApp and we'll set it up for you.

Can I track my delivery?

Yes. The app shows your delivery in real time, so you know exactly when your milk is on its way.

How do I pay?

Pay securely inside the app. If you need help with payment, call or WhatsApp us.

Is there an iPhone app?

The iOS app is coming soon. Until then, iPhone users can order through WhatsApp or by calling us.
